About Saan?

Saan? is a food finder for FEU Tech and FEU Manila students. It helps students choose where to eat around Morayta based on budget, cravings, distance, mood, and available break time.

What It Does

The system lists nearby food spots, filters them by student needs, shows them on a map, saves bookmarks, tracks meal logs, and can randomly suggest a place to eat.

Tracker

The tracker page helps students review meal history, weekly spending, and budget progress without crowding the food finder itself.

Tech Stack

Built with FastAPI, Uvicorn, Jinja2, SQLite, SQLAlchemy, vanilla JavaScript, CSS, Leaflet, and OpenStreetMap.
