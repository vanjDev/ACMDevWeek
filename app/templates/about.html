About Saan?

Saan? is a food finder for FEU Tech and FEU Manila students. It helps students choose where to eat around Morayta based on budget, cravings, walking distance, mood, and available break time.
What it does Privacy Terms Disclaimers Contact

What It Does

The system lists nearby food spots, filters them by student needs, shows stores on a map, saves bookmarks, tracks meal logs, and can randomly suggest a place to eat.

Tracker

The tracker helps students review meal history, weekly spending, budget progress, and tipid feedback without crowding the main food finder.

Tech Stack

Built with FastAPI, Uvicorn, Jinja2, SQLite, SQLAlchemy, vanilla JavaScript, CSS, Leaflet, and OpenStreetMap.

Privacy notice

How Your Data Is Used

Saan? only asks for data that supports food recommendations, saved choices, and the budget tracker.

Data We Store

Saved bookmarks, favorite filters, weekly budget value, and meal history may be stored locally in your browser. If you sign in, these saves can sync to your account.

Location

Location access is optional. If enabled, it is used to estimate distance and walk time from your current position instead of the selected campus.

Meal History

Meal logs use Manila time so daily and weekly spending totals match the local student schedule.

Control

You can clear meal history from the tracker. Guest saves remain on the device unless you sign in and sync them.

Terms of use

Using Saan?

Student Decision Tool

Saan? is designed as a food discovery and budgeting aid. It does not guarantee store availability, exact prices, menu availability, or current operating hours.

Responsible Use

Use recommendations as guidance, then confirm important details directly with the store when needed. Do not rely on the app for emergency, medical, dietary, or financial decisions.

Accounts and Saves

If you create an account, you are responsible for keeping your login credentials private. The account feature is only meant to sync personal saves and preferences.

Content Updates

Restaurant data, photos, prices, routes, and map information may change. The app may update or remove entries to improve accuracy and usability.

Accuracy and third parties

Important Disclaimers

Routes and Walk Times

Walk times are estimates based on coordinates, routing services, or distance calculations. Actual travel time can vary due to crossings, gates, weather, and crowds.

Photos and Sources

Some images and restaurant references may come from public or third-party sources during research. Production use should prioritize permissioned or first-party photos.

External Services

The app may use third-party services such as maps, routing, weather, authentication, and public food data. Those services may have their own terms and privacy practices.

No Official Affiliation

Saan? is a student-focused project and should not be treated as an official FEU, restaurant, or merchant publication unless explicitly stated.

Support and corrections

Found Wrong Data?

If a store, price, route, or photo looks wrong, update it through the project maintainers or admin tools. Accurate local data is the most important part of making Saan? useful.
