About Saan?

Saan? is a food finder for FEU Tech and FEU Manila students. It helps students choose where to eat around Morayta based on budget, cravings, distance, mood, and available break time.

What It Does

The system lists nearby food spots, filters them by student needs, shows them on a map, saves bookmarks, and can randomly suggest a place to eat.

Class Break Helper

The "Can I Make It?" timer helps students check if they have enough time to eat and return before their next class.

Tech Stack

Built with FastAPI, Uvicorn, Jinja2, SQLite, SQLAlchemy, vanilla JavaScript, CSS, Leaflet, and OpenStreetMap.
